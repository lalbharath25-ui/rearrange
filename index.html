How does it work?
  1. Your image and the target are shrunk to exactly the same number of pixels, each keeping its own shape.
  2. Pixels are ranked by brightness and paired: your darkest go to the target's darkest spots, your brightest to its highlights.
  3. Then millions of random swaps are tried, keeping only the ones that bring colours closer. Nothing is painted — each pixel keeps its exact colour; only its position changes.
Every pixel in the result comes from your image — counted, not generated.
